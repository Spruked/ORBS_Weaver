THE SHIFT

From a collection of pages to a living intelligence layer.

Traditional sites make visitors search. Weaver observes the site as a semantic terrain and helps visitors cross it with verified spatial guidance.

The ORB is not a chat window with a face. His body is the interface. His voice is the communication channel. His movement, pointer, and ping are the guidance system.
