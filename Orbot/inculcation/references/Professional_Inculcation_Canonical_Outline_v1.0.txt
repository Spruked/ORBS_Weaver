ORB Professional Inculcation
Canonical outline • Proposed v1.0 • Design only
Purpose
The inculcation is the ORB’s permanent professional education: what the LLM should know to perform its assigned occupation competently. It is not Site World, scan data, or a second governance system.
inculcation.md — training contents
Who am I? — Professional identity, position, character and role.
Where am I? — The type of workplace and how that kind of environment operates; not its live map.
Why am I here? — Purpose and service mission.
What is my job? — Duties, priorities, professional workflows and expectations.
Who do I serve? — Audience needs and standards of service.
What should I know? — Professional knowledge, concepts, vocabulary and methods.
What tools do I have? — Tool purposes, appropriate requests, use cases and interpretation of actual results.
How do I do my job well? — Consultation, problem-solving, conversation, listening, rapport and good judgment.
How do I understand the environment? — How to interpret Site World, scans, pointer and spatial information supplied at runtime.
How do I know I succeeded? — Successful outcomes, incomplete work and truthful result reporting.
How do I improve? — Feedback, verified learning and continuous improvement through the established memory process.
Examples — Practical professional scenarios, good and poor responses, tool-use examples.
Compiled JSON — loader contract
Store inculcation ID, version, source filename, integrity hash, ordered compiled training sections, required-before-interaction flag, retention policy and reload-on-version-change policy. The actual training text must be available to the model—not just a list of section names.
Memory lifecycle
Startup: Verify and load the inculcation before any visitor interaction.
During a session: Keep it as a protected read-only professional-memory partition; visitor conversation lives in separate short-term session memory.
At session end: Purge visitor-specific short-term memory; retain inculcation.
Next session: Reuse the inculcation and create a clean visitor context.
Version change: Revalidate and replace through an authorized update; conversations cannot rewrite it.
Runtime clarification
A persistent application cache does not itself guarantee LLM recall. The inference runtime must attach or reuse the inculcation context for subsequent turns; KV/prefix caching may optimize that reuse.
Boundaries
Site World and scans provide current facts, layout and spatial observations. TPC, Doctrine and the wrapper govern authority and execution. The harness checks performance. Inculcation teaches the profession.
No implementation authorized.
